## 句 1

**英文原句**  
I dropped out of Reed College after the first six months, but then stayed around as a drop-in for another 18 months or so before I really quit.

**中文翻譯**  
我在里德學院讀了六個月後就辦理退學，但此後還以旁聽生的身份待了大約十八個月，才算真正離開。

**英英解釋**  
He left school officially but kept attending classes informally for over a year before truly quitting.

**Key Terms**
1. **drop out** (v.): 退學、中輟
   - syn: quit school | ant: graduate
2. **drop-in** (n.): 旁聽生，非正式出席者
   - syn: informal attendee | ant: enrolled student

**朗讀提示**  
Chunks: I DROPPED OUT | of REED COL-lege | af-ter the FIRST SIX MONTHS. Stress: EIGH-TEEN MONTHS | be-fore I REAL-ly QUIT.

## 句 2

**英文原句**  
So why'd I drop out?

**中文翻譯**  
那麼，我為什麼要退學呢？

**英英解釋**  
He pauses and asks the question that everyone is wondering about.

**Key Terms**
1. **why'd** (contr.): why did 的縮寫口語形式
   - syn: for what reason | ant: (n/a)
2. **drop out** (v.): 退學、中輟
   - syn: quit | ant: stay enrolled

**朗讀提示**  
Rising tone: So WHY'D I drop out? (emphatic question, slight upward pitch)

---

## 句 3

**英文原句**  
It started before I was born.

**中文翻譯**  
這要從我出生之前說起。

**英英解釋**  
The real story of why he dropped out began even before his birth.

**Key Terms**
1. **started** (v.): 開始
   - syn: began | ant: ended
2. **before I was born** (phr.): 在我出生之前
   - syn: prior to my birth | ant: after my birth

**朗讀提示**  
Dramatic, slow: It START-ed | BE-fore I was BORN. (deliberate pause after 'started')

## 句 4

**英文原句**  
My biological mother was a young, unwed graduate student, and she decided to put me up for adoption.

**中文翻譯**  
我的親生母親是一位年輕、未婚的研究生，她決定將我送人收養。

**英英解釋**  
His birth mother was a young unmarried student who chose to give him up for adoption.

**Key Terms**
1. **biological mother** (n.): 親生母親
   - syn: birth mother | ant: adoptive mother
2. **put up for adoption** (phr.): 將孩子送養
   - syn: give up for adoption | ant: keep the child

**朗讀提示**  
Key words: bi-o-LOG-i-cal | un-WED | put me UP for a-DOP-tion. Pause after 'student'.

## 句 5

**英文原句**  
She felt very strongly that I should be adopted by college graduates, so everything was all set for me to be adopted at birth by a lawyer and his wife.

**中文翻譯**  
她非常堅持我必須被大學畢業生收養，所以一切都已安排好，讓我在出生時由一位律師和他的妻子領養。

**英英解釋**  
She insisted that only college graduates could adopt him, and a lawyer couple was already prepared.

**Key Terms**
1. **felt strongly** (phr.): 強烈堅持
   - syn: insisted | ant: felt indifferent
2. **all set** (adj.): 一切就緒，準備好了
   - syn: ready | ant: unprepared

**朗讀提示**  
Emphasis: FELT ver-y STRONG-ly | ALL SET | at BIRTH | by a LAW-yer and his WIFE.

## 句 6

**英文原句**  
Except that when I popped out, they decided at the last minute that they really wanted a girl.

**中文翻譯**  
但就在我出生的那一刻，那對夫妻卻在最後關頭決定，他們其實想要一個女孩。

**英英解釋**  
At the very last moment, the couple changed their mind and wanted a daughter instead.

**Key Terms**
1. **popped out** (phr.): 出生（口語活潑說法）
   - syn: was born | ant: (n/a)
2. **at the last minute** (phr.): 在最後關頭
   - syn: at the very end | ant: in advance

**朗讀提示**  
Casual: when I POPPED OUT | at the LAST MIN-ute | really WANT-ed a GIRL. (light, slightly ironic)

---

## 句 7

**英文原句**  
So my parents, who were on a waiting list, got a call in the middle of the night asking, "We've got an unexpected baby boy. Do you want him?"

**中文翻譯**  
於是，排在等待名單上的我的父母，在半夜接到一通電話，問說：「我們這裡有個意料之外的小男孩，你們要嗎？」

**英英解釋**  
His parents received a surprising late-night phone call offering them an unexpected baby boy.

**Key Terms**
1. **waiting list** (n.): 等候名單
   - syn: queue | ant: immediate placement
2. **unexpected** (adj.): 出乎意料的
   - syn: surprising | ant: expected

**朗讀提示**  
Narrative build: in the MID-dle of the NIGHT | UN-ex-pect-ed BA-by BOY | Do you WANT him? (rising on 'want')

---

## 句 8

**英文原句**  
They said, "Of course."

**中文翻譯**  
他們說：「當然要！」

**英英解釋**  
His parents immediately and warmly agreed to take the baby.

**Key Terms**
1. **of course** (phr.): 當然，毫無疑問
   - syn: absolutely | ant: no
2. **they said** (phr.): 他們回答
   - syn: they responded | ant: they refused

**朗讀提示**  
Warm + instant: They said... 'Of COURSE.' (brief pause before 'Of course', warm rising tone)
